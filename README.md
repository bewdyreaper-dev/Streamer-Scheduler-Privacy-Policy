        <h2><span class="num">01.</span>Who We Are</h2>
        <p>This Privacy Policy describes how Streamer Scheduler (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our website, Discord community, streamer promotions, showcase sign-ups, scheduling, live notifications, and related services (the “Services”).</p>
        <p>Policy contact: <strong>[BewdyReaper, Bewdyreaper@gmail.com]</strong>. If you operate the Services through a registered business, list its appropriate legal name here.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="collect">
        <h2><span class="num">02.</span>Information We May Collect</h2>
        <p>The information we handle depends on which Services you use and which features or integrations are enabled. It may include:</p>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Account and profile information:</strong> Discord username, user ID where accessible to the bot, streamer name, profile or channel URL, and other details you choose to share.</li>
          <li><strong>Sign-up and scheduling details:</strong> selected showcase dates and times, time zone, slot preferences, participation status, and event-related messages.</li>
          <li><strong>Messages and reports:</strong> information you send to moderators or provide through tickets, support requests, feedback, or reports.</li>
          <li><strong>Technical and usage information:</strong> limited logs or diagnostic information made available by the hosting provider, Discord, or tools we use to operate the Services.</li>
          <li><strong>Public promotional content:</strong> channel links, stream titles, announcements, and promotional material you choose to submit for display in the community.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Do not collect or ask for sensitive information unless there is a clear, lawful need and suitable safeguards. We do not intend to collect payment-card details through this website unless a separate, secure payment process is introduced and explained.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="use">
        <h2><span class="num">03.</span>How We Use Information</h2>
        <p>Where applicable and permitted by law, information may be used to:</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Manage community membership, event sign-ups, showcase rosters, and schedules.</li>
          <li>Post requested promotional announcements, reminders, or live notifications.</li>
          <li>Respond to questions, support requests, reports, and moderation concerns.</li>
          <li>Apply community rules, prevent spam or abuse, and help protect members and services.</li>
          <li>Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of our Services.</li>
          <li>Meet legal obligations and protect our rights, members, or operations where appropriate.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>Where a privacy law requires a specific legal basis for processing, we will identify and rely on an applicable basis, such as providing a requested service, complying with a legal obligation, consent, or legitimate interests where legally permitted.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="sharing">
        <h2><span class="num">04.</span>When Information Is Shared</h2>
        <p>We do not sell personal information for money. Information may be visible to other members when you post it in public Discord channels or submit it for a public promotional listing. Please consider what you choose to publish.</p>
        <p>We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the Services, such as website hosting, Discord bots, scheduling tools, or notification integrations. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, address security or abuse concerns, or protect rights and safety. If ownership or operations change, information may be transferred as permitted by law with appropriate safeguards.</p>
        <p>Only name providers and sharing practices that accurately describe your actual setup. Do not promise that information is never shared if your bots or integrations process it.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="platforms">
        <h2><span class="num">05.</span>Discord, Twitch, Bots & External Platforms</h2>
        <p>Our Services may rely on Discord, Twitch, GitHub Pages, bots, and other third-party services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Their availability, permissions, and data practices are not controlled entirely by Streamer Scheduler.</p>
        <p>Review the policies for any third-party service you use. We may receive information made available through authorized integrations, such as channel details or live status, only to the extent those integrations are configured to provide it. Do not list integrations that you do not use.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="retention">
        <h2><span class="num">06.</span>How Long Information Is Kept</h2>
        <p>We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including managing schedules, resolving disputes, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by information type and by the settings of third-party providers.</p>
        <p>To request deletion of information we control, use the contact details below. We may need to retain some information where legally required or where a permitted exception applies. Removing a Discord post does not necessarily remove copies, logs, or records held by Discord or third-party services.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="security">
        <h2><span class="num">07.</span>Security</h2>
        <p>We use reasonable measures appropriate to our setup to help protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No website, online service, Discord server, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to moderation records, bot permissions, and account credentials should be limited to people who need them to operate the Services.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="choices">
        <h2><span class="num">08.</span>Your Choices & Privacy Rights</h2>
        <p>Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Some rights are subject to exceptions and verification requirements under applicable law.</p>
        <p>You can choose not to provide optional information, leave the community, or stop submitting promotional sign-ups. Some features may not work without the information needed to provide them. To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We will respond as required by applicable law.</p>
        <p>You may also need to manage information directly with Discord, Twitch, or another third-party provider under that provider’s own processes.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="children">
        <h2><span class="num">09.</span>Children & Age Requirements</h2>
        <p>Our Services are intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any additional age restrictions announced for particular channels or activities. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate steps.</p>
      </section>

      <section class="term" id="transfers">

        <p>Discord, hosting providers, and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers. The actual locations and safeguards depend on the providers and configuration used by Streamer Scheduler.</p>
      </section>
